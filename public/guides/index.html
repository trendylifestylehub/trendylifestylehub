Buying Guides

Useful checklists to help compare products before you buy.

Choosing a handbag

  • Check dimensions and weight.
  • Review material and care instructions.
  • Look at compartments, closures and strap length.
  • Read the retailer’s returns policy.

Choosing skincare

  • Read the full ingredient list.
  • Check suitability and warnings on the label.
  • Buy from a retailer you trust.
  • Do not rely on unsupported product claims.
